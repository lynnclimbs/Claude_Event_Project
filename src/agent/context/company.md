Schraube GmbH is a mid-sized German manufacturer of industrial fasteners —
screws, bolts, threaded rods and custom fastening solutions. Founded 1962,
based in Baden-Württemberg, roughly 180 employees.

## What we sell

- Standard DIN/ISO fasteners in steel, stainless and specialty alloys
- Custom-machined fasteners to customer drawings
- Consignment stock and Kanban resupply programmes

## Who we sell to

German B2B Mittelstand — machine builders, automotive suppliers, plant
engineering firms. Typical deal is a recurring supply agreement, not a one-off
purchase. Buying committees are small (2–4 people) but decisions are slow and
relationship-driven.

## How we compete

We are not the cheapest. We win on delivery reliability, technical advice on
material and coating selection, and willingness to take small custom batches
that larger suppliers refuse.

## What we never claim

- Never quote a firm price or delivery date — that requires a human.
- Never commit to a tolerance or certification without engineering sign-off.
